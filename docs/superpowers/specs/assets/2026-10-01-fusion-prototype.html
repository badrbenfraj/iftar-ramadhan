  1. Pick a language on Welcome, then sign in. Leave a field empty to see the error.
  2. Open the scanner (round mint button), scan Fatma, confirm, then press Undo.
  3. Scan Mohamed to see "already served".

  5. Tap "Find someone without a card" and type "fat".
  6. Scan the unknown card, then register it. In Add, type CIN 08123812.
  7. Close the scanner to see tonight's summary.

What comes from where

From Maghrib light

  • The palette: night sky, lantern gold, mint on night, teal on day, limestone, clay
  • Welcome: arch window, crescent, dusk horizon, calligraphy
  • People: night header with the 8-point star, cards with gold ID chips
  • Night tab bar with the mint scan button

From the UX prototype

  • Instant identify, one-tap confirm, 5 s Undo
  • Honest offline mode, plus "serve offline" with sync
  • Find without a card, with a CIN check
  • Scan to register, steppers, duplicate CIN
  • Waiting filter, instant stats, tonight's summary

From Zitouna and the critiques

  • The 8-point-star seal carries each scan verdict
  • Hand-over quantities in large numbers
  • Icon plus word in every status, never color alone
  • A fixed maghrib time, not a countdown
  • Masked CIN, and translation into 3 languages
